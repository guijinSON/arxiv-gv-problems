# TASK: make G5 report difficulty, not cardinality

`submit.sh` refuses this module: G5 is missing a density and a baseline cost.
Reporting the size of the candidate space is not evidence of difficulty -- another
family reported a 1536-bit space, passed G5, and fell to Algorithm X in five
seconds. A third reported P(guess)=0/200000 and lost to WalkSAT in 3.65 seconds.

Add BOTH to the G5 entry of `selftest_report.json`, measured at the SHIPPING preset:

1. **Density** -- how many answers are actually valid. Exact count if `enumerate_all`
   terminates at the shipping preset; otherwise sample `random_candidate` and report
   the observed valid fraction with the sample size. A count from a smaller preset
   may be reported in addition, labelled with its n, never instead.

2. **Baseline cost** -- run your strongest G6 attack at the shipping preset and
   record what it cost: wall-clock seconds and iterations/nodes/restarts, and
   whether it succeeded.

Use key names containing "fraction"/"density"/"valid"/"solutions"/"count" and
"seconds"/"nodes"/"iterations"/"restarts" so the check can find them.

Re-run `selftest()`, overwrite `selftest_report.json`, and update the G5 row of
`README.md` with the measured numbers.

**If the baseline cost shows your strongest attack SOLVES the shipping preset, say
so and fail the gate.** That is the finding this measurement exists to surface --
do not tune the preset to hide it. Change nothing about the mathematics of the
family; you are adding measurements.
